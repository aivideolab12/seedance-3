# Seedance 3.0 Prompt Examples

A small collection of prompts and tips for [Seedance 3.0](https://seedance3.si/), an AI video model with smooth motion and strong prompt adherence.

## Tips
- Keep prompts short and concrete.
- Describe camera movement (slow dolly in, handheld pan).
- Keep the subject description identical across clips for consistent characters.
- For image-to-video, use a clean, well-lit reference frame.

## Example prompts
- A woman walking through a neon-lit alley in the rain, slow dolly in, cinematic.
- A golden retriever running on a beach at sunset, low tracking shot.
- Steam rising from a cup of coffee on a wooden table, macro, soft morning light.

Try these online with [seedance 3](https://seedance3.si/).
